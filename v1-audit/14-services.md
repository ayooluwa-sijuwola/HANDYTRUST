## Structural finding: at least three services exist purely to duplicate another service's job-mutation logic for the demo/legacy path
`EscrowService`, `DemoEscrowService`, and `PaymentService` collectively represent three different historical attempts at a payment/escrow write path, none of which is the actual production path (`JobService` + the Cloud Functions in Part 8). This is the clearest instance in the whole services layer of the "one canonical writer per collection" principle (stated in `JobService`'s own header comment) not being fully followed in practice.
